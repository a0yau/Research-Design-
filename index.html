Did the Quota Move the Needle?

What I want to find out: whether Kazakhstan's 2021 combined gender-youth quota changed how much legislation women MPs initiate in the Mazhilis.

Proposal · Positioning & Theory by smthelse2

Research question

Did the quota affect women MPs' legislative output?

Did the 30% combined gender-youth quota (introduced 2021) affect the average number of laws initiated per woman MP in Kazakhstan's Mazhilis, comparing the 2016–2021 session (pre-quota) to the 2021–2026 session (post-quota)?

Why here, why now

Why I think it's worth asking

  • Combined gender-youth-disability quotas are becoming common outside Western democracies, but are rarely studied there.
  • Kazakhstan's quota doesn't guarantee more women — parties can meet it with youth or disability candidates alone.
  • Whether representation gains translate into legislative behavior remains untested here.
Epistemology · positioning

I work in the positivist tradition

Positivists treat the social world as measurable and look for patterns across cases (Farrow et al. 2020).

  • My question is cause and effect, with a direction.
  • I compare women MPs' legislative activity before and after the quota.
Literature · the map

Two camps, one shared limitation

Critical mass theorists

More women present changes institutional behavior, not just composition.

Institutional skeptics

Quotas are largely symbolic, especially in centralized assemblies.

Both camps draw almost entirely on Western democratic assemblies.

Literature · the gap

Two gaps, not one

Data-gapIndividual-level bill authorship in the Mazhilis has never been catalogued.
Test-gapBecause that data doesn't exist, the quota's causal effect on women's legislative output has never been tested — anywhere in Central Asia.
Theory

Theory first, then test

Introducing a legislative gender quota increases the substantive legislative output of women MPs, because structural inclusion lowers barriers to agenda-setting — giving women both the critical mass and institutional legitimacy needed to sponsor legislation.

Deductive design: theory → hypothesis → test.

The mechanism · causal chain

How the quota is supposed to work

Quota raises women's seat share
→
Less tokenism, more networks
→
Institutional leverage
→
More bills initiated per MP

Step 1 must be verified empirically, not assumed — the quota is a combined women/youth/disability category.
